About FloodSense AI

FloodSense AI is an intelligent flood risk prediction system that combines weather analysis, terrain data, river proximity, and AI-generated insights to estimate flood danger levels in nearly real time.

Project Overview

FloodSense AI helps users analyze flood-prone areas using live environmental data and artificial intelligence. Users can select any location directly on the map to receive flood risk analysis, confidence scores, weather insights, and AI-powered safety suggestions.

How It Works

1. Select Location

Users choose a location on the interactive map.

2. Data Collection

The system fetches live weather, forecast, elevation, and river proximity data.

3. Risk Analysis

Flood risk is calculated using a weighted environmental risk model.

4. AI Insights

Gemini AI generates flood reasoning and safety recommendations.

Why FloodSense AI Matters

Floods are one of the most common natural disasters in India. According to the National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA), more than 40 million hectares of land in India are flood-prone, and thousands of lives are affected every year.
